About Me

The Mind Behind The Mission

I'm Gaurav Koirala, an energetic educator at Sunsari Madhesha Academy, teaching Science, Computer, and Health to students of Grade 5 through Grade 8. In just over a year in the classroom, I've built a teaching style rooted in curiosity, experiments, and honest conversation — the kind that turns a shy question into a confident idea.

My philosophy is simple: children learn best when they are allowed to touch, break, question, and rebuild. Whether it's a chemical reaction, a line of code, or a lesson on healthy habits, I design every class to be hands-on and human-first.

My mission is to make Science feel like discovery, Computers feel like creation, and Health feel like self-respect — not subjects to memorize, but tools to live by. My vision is a generation of students at Sunsari Madhesha Academy who leave my classroom more curious than they entered it.
